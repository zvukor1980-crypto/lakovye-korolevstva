# Dawn artwork

Original AI-generated game artwork, created with the built-in image generator, 2026-11-04. No external photographs or music recordings.

- menu-dawn.webp: cinematic portrait matte painting, ivory castle, river valley, Inca sun temple, two fictional adult women commanders in fully covered glossy burgundy and black-gold armor, quiet sky and terrace for UI, no text.
- grass/dirt/sand/snow/water/rock.webp: six equal overhead terrain panels from a generated 3×2 atlas, fine natural texture, consistent light, no buildings or perspective. Extracted into individual WebP textures.
- battle-valley.webp: broad empty meadow and weathered flagstone foreground, distant castle, waterfalls and mountain valley, no figures/grid/text.

Images compressed for mobile. The title-screen mist and map ripples/birds are procedural animation, not generated video. The chamber music is an original procedural synthesis in js/music.js, not a recording of live musicians. Optional speech uses an available Russian device voice; its gender and offline availability depend on the device.
